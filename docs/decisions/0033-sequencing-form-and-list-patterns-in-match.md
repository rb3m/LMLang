# ADR-033 Sequencing form `(do ...)` and list patterns in `match` (2026-10-16)

Context: ADR-025 and AGYS_OPINION.md identified two ergonomic causes of token bloat in the benchmark outliers (`13-greet` at 2.64x, `10-roman` at 2.34x): (1) sequencing side effects required dummy `(let ((u (io.print ...))) ...)` bindings with invented local names because of ADR-016 (no shadowing), and (2) iterating over lists with effects or table data required manual index recursion with `list.nth` and `try/catch OutOfRange` because function values must be pure and total (ADR-015) and `match` had no list destructuring.
Decision:
1. `(do e1 e2 ... en)` (special form, `n >= 1`; `do` added to reserved words): evaluates `e1..en` left to right and returns the value and type of `en`. Every preceding expression `e1..e(n-1)` must have type `Unit` (checked via `expect(UNIT, ...)`, reporting `E-TYPE` if a non-`Unit` value is silently discarded). Effects and failures of all sub-expressions flow through the standard checker accumulators.
2. List patterns in `match`: `(list)` matches the empty list, `(list p1 ... pk)` matches a list of exact length `k`, and `(cons h t)` matches a non-empty list with head `h` and tail `t`. Exhaustiveness follows SPEC section 12's conservative rule: `(list)` and `(cons _ _)` with irrefutable sub-patterns cover `(List T)`; exact-length `(list p1 ... pk)` ($k \ge 1$) patterns or nested refutable sub-patterns require `(cons _ _)` or a catch-all `_`.
Result (`lm bench` after rewriting `13-greet` and `10-roman`, and retargeting `roman:unknown-local-fn` in `lm/bench.py` from `build` to `step`):
- `13-greet` dropped from 222 to 148 tokens (2.64x -> **1.76x** vs full Python), eliminating the helper function `go`, `list.nth`, `OutOfRange`, and dummy `let`.
- `10-roman` dropped from 473 to 282 tokens (2.34x -> **1.40x** vs full Python), replacing 14 nested `if` forms with a table-driven step over value/symbol lists.
- Headline S5 median across the 13 tasks remains **1.34x** vs full Python (3.00x vs bare Python; sensitivity with merged punctuation runs improved from 1.38x to **1.34x**), because the median task (rank 7 of 13, `05-collatz` at 1.34x) did not change while the two worst right-tail outliers moved from 2.64x/2.34x to 1.76x/1.40x. The S5 gate (`<= 1.3x`) remains **OPEN** (not passed, per ADR-025). S1 (4.3% project, 54.2% module), S2 (9.8%), S3 (43/43 = 100%), S4 (13/13 = 100%), and S6 (6/6, guard OK) all hold.
Revisit: if multi-branch boolean dispatch still bloats non-list tasks, consider `(cond ...)` or module-level constants.
