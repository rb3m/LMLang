31. (Done 2026-11-25, ADR-082: `http.request-within` with a total deadline of 1..600 s; `request`/`fetch` keep 30 s; `local-llm` generated 1,500 tokens from a cold load in one 66 s call.) (Queued 2026-11-25, owner request.) HTTP client deadline: raise the fixed 30-second total deadline (ADR-069 R3, "fixed for v0") or make it configurable. Evidence: the `local-llm` helper (an LM program that sends small jobs to a local Ollama model, part of the owner's coordinator skill) can't finish long jobs. On an M4 with 16 GB, a cold model load takes 10–14 s and a 1,419-token reply from gemma4:e4b took 52 s, so it now loads the model in a separate first call, caps replies at about 600 tokens and exits on `timeout`. Streaming wouldn't help: the deadline covers the whole body. Options for an ADR and consensus: (1) a higher fixed default; (2) a per-call deadline, e.g. `http.request-with` taking an options record with `timeout-ms` under a hard cap; (3) an idle/read timeout in place of, or as well as, the total deadline (best with streaming responses); (4) separate connect, first-byte and total limits. Must stay identical in the interpreter, Go and Python targets, with deadline parity tests. Acceptance: `local-llm` runs a 1,500-token generation without the preload workaround. Notes and measurements: Vault, `SoftwareDevelopment/AI/20261125-0510 LMLang HTTP Deadline and Local LLM Jobs.md`.
